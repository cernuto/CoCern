Paul authorized all four follow-up optimizations from the November 11 live database comparison, starting with smaller native probe and prompt-hook replies. The changes are implemented on `codex/background-efficiency-followup`, based on `1fc2bdd`.

| Area | Implemented behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Native probes and prompt hooks | Fixed `probe` and `prompt` status projections preserve fresh authorization, exact session identity, tool settings and pause/cancellation controls. Prompt replies also carry attention counts, compact top-item identity and standing revision. Unused dashboard, history, binding, order and offer-preview content is omitted. |
| Idle polling | Per-route deadlines use five minutes for a proven idle subscribed route, 60 seconds for pending attention and 15 seconds for errors or disconnected channels. Pending routes do not force idle neighbors to poll. Targeted hints, inventory changes and reconnect recovery interrupt appropriately; an independent full-pass deadline cannot be postponed by hint or retry traffic. |
| Background telemetry | `Relay:Activity:BackgroundRetentionDays` defaults to 1; 0 disables early rollup. Whole completed UTC days of successful metadata-free background status reads roll up transactionally into daily traffic/origin totals and bounded per-session/hour measurements. At least the latest 24 hours remain raw. |
| Completed task history | `Relay:Board:ArchiveAfterDays` now defaults to 1 instead of 30. Existing safe archiving, archive read-through, search, ID/replay fences and delta removals remain in use. Explicit configuration still overrides the default. |

Projection envelopes reject conditional status tags and attached mutations, so probes cannot return an unchanged-only reply or acknowledge unseen content. The controls captured by `CallControls` are passed explicitly through `ToolRouter` to `StatusTools`; raw internal projection fields are overwritten rather than trusted. Fresh `WithToolSettingsAsync` processing still supplies session identity and the administrator ceiling. Ordinary status and explicit manager-history reads retain their existing behavior.

Quiet polling requires fresh permitted status with no inbox or pending attention, obtained while the exact subscription is listening. A cached idle verdict is usable only after the hub freshly confirms its exact known validator. Missing or malformed evidence retains the shorter pace. Known pending/snoozed/held work keeps the existing cadence, and dispatch still performs its fresh control check. Changed private handles or route identity clear the previous deadline and reconcile immediately. Explicit caller intervals remain honored.

Early telemetry eligibility excludes failures, foreground or unclassified calls, manager reads, mutations and records carrying reported usage, target, content-hash, workflow or offer metadata. Daily rollup, origin rollup, hourly measurements and raw deletion share one transaction. Retries cannot count a row twice. Hourly measurements expire at the ordinary retention cutoff; daily traffic totals retain the existing lifetime. Weighted analytics preserve token totals and sample counts, per-session context, compaction denominators and session-hour outliers. Older summarized measurements have UTC-hour precision. Snapshot conversion supplies an empty compatibility table for databases predating the new summary table, and status-only rollups no longer falsely mark workflow history missing.
